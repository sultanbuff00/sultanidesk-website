New · Service 02

WhatsApp Omni Service — orders & support, handled

Many customers don't call — they message on WhatsApp. This service puts an AI assistant on your WhatsApp Business number, day and night, so no message or WhatsApp call is left waiting — and it helps each customer choose the right product from your catalogue, so buying feels easy instead of confusing.

What the WhatsApp AI does

  • Takes order details for you. Item, quantity, customer name, delivery address and callback number — written down clearly and sent to you to confirm and fulfil.
  • Answers everyday questions. Hours, address, products and policies — only from the information you give us about your business.
  • Records complaints properly. It replies politely, notes what happened, and passes the complaint to you. It never approves or refuses a refund, return or compensation — that decision is always yours.
  • Guides customers through your catalogue. If a customer isn't sure what they want, it asks what kind of thing they're looking for — the type of item, who it's for, their budget if they want to share it, and preferences like colour or size — then suggests matching items from your own catalogue, at your catalogue prices. No long back-and-forth: the customer sees a short, clear shortlist and can order straight away.
  • Suggests by need, occasion and taste. It listens for why they're buying and who it's for — a gift for a mother, wife, husband, children or a friend; an occasion like Eid, a wedding, a birthday or a new home; or something for everyday use — and how the customer likes to choose. Then it suggests 1–3 products from your catalogue that fit that need and occasion best, with a brief reason for each one, so the customer feels understood and can decide with confidence.
  • Sends the product's Shopify link. When it suggests a product — or the customer would rather buy online — it sends that product's link from your own catalogue, exactly as you provided it, never an invented or shortened one. The customer taps through to your Shopify website, orders and pays there through the online checkout. Payment is taken safely by Shopify — the AI never handles card or payment details in the chat. The customer chooses: buy on the website via the link, or order inside the chat.
  • Works day and night. Messages and WhatsApp calls are answered 24/7, even when the shop is closed.
Clear limits, on purpose: suggestions are never pushy — there is no pressure, no false urgency, and a “no” is accepted at once. The AI never invents a price, discount, stock level, delivery date or product feature, never takes card numbers or passwords, and says “I'm not sure” instead of guessing. If nothing in your catalogue fits what the customer needs, it says so honestly and takes a message for you instead. Order details are notes for you to check — please verify them before fulfilling an order.

Delivery & Shipping Setup — so orders can go out

A website that takes orders is only half the job — the parcel still has to reach the customer. This service connects your store to a shipping provider, in your own name, and sets everything up so you can print a label, stick it on the parcel and send the order on its way.

What the setup includes

  • Your own shipping provider account. An account with a shipping provider (such as a label service like Pirate Ship, or your carrier of choice) opened in your name and your business details — it is your account, and postage is bought through it at the provider's rates.
  • Connected to your store. The shipping account is linked to your Shopify store, so an order's customer name and delivery address flow straight into the label — no re-typing addresses by hand.
  • Return address set. Your store's address is set as the sender and return address on every label, so an undelivered parcel comes back to you, not lost.
  • Shipping zones & rates set. The areas you deliver to — for example the whole US, or only certain states — and what the customer pays for shipping in each zone, set from the shipping policy you provide: flat rate, by weight, or calculated at checkout, as your store supports.
  • Label printing set up. Your printer is set up and checked for shipping labels, so printing a label from an order takes a few clicks.
  • One test label, run together. We run one complete test label with you, the owner — from a real order screen to a printed label — so you see exactly how it works before your first real order goes out.
Clear costs, stated plainly: the $149 is our one-time setup fee — there is no monthly fee from us for this service.
